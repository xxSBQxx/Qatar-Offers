Cookies & Local Storage

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Qatar time (Asia/Qatar)

Current configuration: no third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or social embeds are enabled by default. The site uses local browser storage for functionality and an essential session cookie when signing in.

1. What is stored

  • Session cookie: when using the backend account system, a signed HttpOnly cookie keeps a user signed in. It is marked SameSite=Strict; the Secure flag must be enabled in HTTPS production.
  • Local storage: language selection, favorite offer IDs, recently viewed offer IDs, and this cookie-choice preference are saved on the visitor's device.
  • Server database: account sessions and favorites are stored by the backend when signed-in features are used.

2. Choice controls

The website's banner lets you choose “Use essentials only” or “Continue”. There are currently no optional analytics or advertising cookies to activate. Choosing essentials only does not disable local storage required for the website's language, favorites, and recently viewed features; those are functional preferences and can be cleared from your browser. If optional tracking is added in the future, it must remain disabled until the appropriate choice is obtained.

3. Third-party resources

The application does not intentionally embed third-party social widgets. Offer images may be loaded from merchant source URLs; those image hosts may receive a normal browser request. Clicking an external source link takes you to that provider, which has its own privacy and cookie policies.

4. How to clear local data

Use your browser's site settings to clear cookies and site data. Clearing local storage removes local favorites, language preference, and recently viewed history. Signing out removes the server session cookie. Account favorites stored on the server remain associated with the account until removed or the account is deleted.

5. Legal review

Cookie and local-storage obligations vary by deployment, visitor location, and technologies used. This page describes the current project configuration; re-audit it before adding analytics, advertising, embedded social content, or additional third-party scripts.
