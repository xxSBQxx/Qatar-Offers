Cookie Policy

Last reviewed: 12 October 2026

What we actually use today

This site sets no cookies as of this review. Instead it uses your browser's localStorage — a different, non-cookie storage mechanism that is not sent to any server on every request the way a cookie is — for a short list of strictly necessary, on-device functions:

KeyPurposeConsent needed?
qo_langRemembers your English/Arabic choiceNo — strictly necessary
qo_themeRemembers light/dark modeNo — strictly necessary
qo_favoritesOffers you've saved, on this deviceNo — strictly necessary (core feature)
qo_recentOffers you've recently viewed, on this deviceNo — strictly necessary (core feature)
qo_profileAn optional local display nameNo — you opt in by typing it
qo_cookie_consentRemembers your answer to this bannerNo — required to respect your choice

None of these are shared with any third party, used for advertising, or combined with anything to identify you personally. Under both general international guidance (e.g. the EU's ePrivacy rules) and the spirit of Qatar's PDPPL, storage that is strictly necessary to provide a feature you asked for does not require opt-in consent — which is why the banner you may have seen offers "Necessary only" as a real, fully-functional option, not a dark pattern.

What we don't use (yet)

There are currently no analytics cookies/scripts (e.g. Google Analytics), no advertising cookies, and no third-party embeds that set cookies on this site. If that changes in the future — for example, to add aggregate, privacy-respecting usage analytics — it will only load after you choose "Accept" in the cookie banner, this page will be updated to name the specific tool and what it collects, and "Necessary only" will continue to work exactly as before.

Your choice

You can change your answer at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings (which also clears your favorites and preferences), or — once implemented — via a "Manage cookie choice" control the operator should add here alongside the real analytics tool name, if one is ever added.

Third-party links

Offer "source" links take you to each mall or store's own website, which has its own cookies and privacy practices outside our control. The same applies to this site's admin dashboard, which loads no third-party scripts.

This page should be reviewed and updated by the site operator whenever the site's actual cookie/analytics footprint changes — it is written to match the codebase as shipped, not as a generic template.
