Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Qatar time (Asia/Qatar)

Transparency note: Qatar Offers is currently configured not to load advertising pixels, third-party analytics, or social-media embeds. Before launch, the operator should replace this template with the verified legal identity and contact channel of the actual operator.

1. Who operates this service

This website is an offer-discovery directory. The legal name, registered address, and contact details of the operator have not been provided in the project configuration, so they are intentionally not invented here. Do not launch user accounts publicly until a real privacy contact and responsible operator identity have been supplied.

2. Information processed

  • Account data, when account features are enabled: username, password hash, account role, creation time, last login, and session records.
  • Favorites associated with a signed-in account.
  • Security and administration records such as login events, job errors, moderation actions, and timestamps.
  • Local browser storage for language preference, favorites, recently viewed offers, and cookie-choice preference.
  • Basic request metadata may be processed by the hosting provider or server infrastructure; hosting logs and retention must be checked for the chosen deployment.

Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2 hashes, not plaintext. The application does not need date of birth, precise location, payment-card details, or contact-list access.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Data is used to provide account and favorite features, protect the service, administer moderation, troubleshoot failures, and retain a security/audit trail. The operator must confirm an appropriate legal basis and notices for the actual deployment. For Qatar, review the Personal Data Privacy Protection Law No. 13 of 2016 and any applicable decisions and guidance.

4. Cookies, analytics and third parties

The backend uses an HttpOnly session cookie when account authentication is enabled. The current frontend uses browser localStorage for interface preferences, favorites, and recently viewed offers. No analytics or advertising integrations are enabled by default. Offer links and images may load from merchant websites when a visitor chooses to open them or when an offer image is displayed; those providers may receive normal browser request data. The operator should review hosting logs, image hotlinking, external links, and any future integrations before launch.

5. Retention, access, correction and deletion

Offer data is retained to support verification history and administration. User accounts and favorites remain until the account is deleted or the operator removes them. Audit logs may retain a minimal record of security-relevant actions after account deletion to protect the service; configure and document a proportionate retention period before public launch. Signed-in users can request account deletion through the backend account endpoint; where that control is not exposed in the UI, contact the verified operator once published. Do not place real personal information in issue trackers or public reports.

6. Security and transfers

The server implementation provides password hashing, role checks, rate limits, session expiry, same-site HttpOnly cookies, origin checks for write requests, and security response headers. Deployment security also depends on HTTPS, secrets, server configuration, backups, patching, and access controls. GitHub-hosted infrastructure or any future hosting provider may process data in other jurisdictions; the operator must verify the actual provider and applicable transfer requirements.

7. Children and sensitive data

The service is not designed to request sensitive personal data. Do not submit health, religious, biometric, financial-account, or children's personal data through feedback or account fields.

8. Rights and complaints

Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data and to raise complaints. The operator must add a monitored privacy contact before accepting public accounts. Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law No. 13 of 2016 is available through the official Al Meezan legal portal.

9. Legal review required

This notice is a technical drafting baseline, not a certification of compliance. A qualified Qatar legal adviser should review the operator identity, lawful bases, retention schedule, cross-border processing, security incident handling, children's access, and any target-market laws before launch. The EU/UK GDPR or other international privacy rules may apply depending on the operator’s establishment, targeting, and processing context; global accessibility alone is not enough to conclude that a law applies or does not apply.

Qatar Offers is an independent offer-discovery directory. Merchants control their own offers and sales. This page is an operational policy template and is not a legal opinion.
