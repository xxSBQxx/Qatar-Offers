3. Legal basis and your rights under Qatari law

Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law, Law No. 13 of 2016 ("PDPPL"), governs the processing of personal data in Qatar and generally requires consent as the primary legal basis for processing, along with rights of access, correction, and objection for data subjects. Because this site does not currently collect personal data beyond what is described in Section 2 (which stays on your own device), there is little for the PDPPL's data-subject rights to act on today — but if the operator later adds accounts, newsletters, contact forms, or analytics that identify individuals, those features must each be assessed against the PDPPL (and, for visitors based elsewhere, against any applicable law in their own country, such as the GDPR for EU/EEA visitors) before launch, with this policy updated accordingly.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local-storage data (favorites, recently viewed, preferences, consent record) persists only on your device until you clear your browser's site data, or until the operator ships a feature to clear it in-app. Offer data is retained only while an offer is live, plus a short grace window to tolerate temporary source-site outages, and is removed automatically once its stated expiry date passes or it has not been confirmed by any source for 10 days (see the Terms of Use for how expiry is determined). There is no personal data to delete on request today; if that changes, this section will describe a deletion request process and a named contact.

6. Security

This project keeps all API keys and credentials (for example, any AI-classification or search-discovery API key) in environment variables / repository secrets, never committed to source control or exposed in client-side code. The admin dashboard's passphrase is a basic access deterrent appropriate to a static, serverless site; it is not represented as securing personal data, because none is collected there.
